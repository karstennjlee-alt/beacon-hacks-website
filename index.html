Show up alone. Leave with something you built.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

01What even is a hackathon?

A room full of people building something small and finishing it in a day: an app, a game, a website, a hardware hack. Winning is beside the point. Shipping something with a few other people is the point.

02Do I need to know how to code?

No. Plenty of attendees write their first real code at Beacon. Mentors are on the floor from 10am and the First Light track judges effort and learning, not polish.

03What grades can apply?

High school, grades 9 through 12, anywhere in the Bay Area. Bring a school ID for check in.

04Solo or team?

Teams of one to four. Come alone and the team matching session right after check in will sort you out.

05Is it actually free?

Yes. Sponsors cover the venue, the food and the prizes so nobody pays to build.

06Do I have to stay overnight?

Never. Doors at 8:30am, awards done by 11pm, everyone sleeps in their own bed.

07How do I mentor or sponsor?

Email team@beaconhacks.org about mentoring or sponsors@beaconhacks.org about sponsoring. We answer the same day.
